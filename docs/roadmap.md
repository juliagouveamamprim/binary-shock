# Roadmap

Binary Shock will keep a traceable distinction between quantities calculated
by IBSEn, physically motivated reconstructions, and artistic presentation.

## Next scientific layer

- Map spatial emissivity in selected X-ray and gamma-ray energy bands.
- Include relativistic Doppler boosting in the observer-facing brightness.
- Preserve the raw Doppler-factor view as a diagnostic mode rather than the
  final cinematic color mapping.

## Cinematic interpretation

- Add controlled filaments, turbulence, and apparent shell thickness over the
  analytic geometry.
- Label those details as physically plausible artistic interpretation, not as
  hydrodynamic or magnetohydrodynamic simulation output.
- Use the IBSEn magnetic-field magnitude on the shock as a quantitative visual
  input where appropriate.

## System orientation

- Show the orbital angular-momentum axis and the Be-star rotation axis, using
  the disk normal as the latter under an explicit alignment assumption.
- Add configurable pulsar spin and magnetic axes; these are not supplied by
  IBSEn and must be literature-constrained or marked as assumed.
- Represent the pulsed magnetospheric beams as an optional explanatory layer,
  separate from the phase-averaged pulsar wind and unpulsed shock emission.

## Magnetic-field interpretation

- Treat IBSEn field values as scalar magnitudes, not vector field lines.
- If dipolar, toroidal, or striped-wind field lines are shown, identify them as
  a separate physically motivated reconstruction.
